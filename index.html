Root Analyser – Numerical Methods Root Solver

By Snehil Pandey

Root Analyser is a free online numerical methods calculator that solves nonlinear equations using Bisection, Regula-Falsi, Newton-Raphson, and Secant methods with step-by-step visualization.

The tool helps students and teachers understand iterative root finding algorithms used in numerical analysis, engineering mathematics, and scientific computing.

Root Analyser provides an interactive calculator and step-by-step visualization, making it ideal for students, teachers, and anyone learning numerical analysis or engineering mathematics.
